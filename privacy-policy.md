# Privacy Policy — T-CAN and T-CAN Lite

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

T-CAN and T-CAN Lite ("the app") are Android applications that read vehicle CAN-bus data from an OBD Bluetooth adapter (ELM327-compatible, vLinker, OBDLink) and display it on the phone.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no backend server and no user accounts. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data or vehicle data with the developer or any third party.

## Data stored on your device

Everything the app works with stays on your phone:

- **Vehicle data** read from the CAN bus is shown live and, if you enable the CAN logger (full version only), can be saved as a log file in your Downloads folder at your request.
- **Dashboard snapshots** (full version only) are saved to Downloads or shared through the Android share sheet only when you tap the camera button.
- **Settings and preferences** (selected car profile, dashboard layout, theme, language) are stored locally in the app's private storage.

You can delete all of this by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Log files and snapshots in Downloads are ordinary files you manage yourself.

## Permissions

- **Bluetooth (Nearby devices)** — required to discover and connect to the OBD adapter. The app never uses Bluetooth to determine your location (`neverForLocation`).
- **Location (Android 11 and older only)** — older Android versions require this permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read, store or transmit your location.
- **Storage (Android 10 and older only)** — to save log files and snapshots to Downloads at your request.
- **Internet** — used only to open external links you tap (Google Play page, source credits). The app itself makes no network requests.

## Third-party services

The app does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting or any other third-party data-collecting service. Payment for the full version is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
